# Motion Reference Notes

## Source

[1]: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1IbDmG4lpR0 — Cinematic Typography + Sound Design, analyzed on 2026-08-18.

## Useful Observations

The reference uses rapid, beat-matched edits, kinetic stacked typography, hard cuts, high-contrast black/white flips, textured footage overlays, and punctuation from whooshes and low-frequency impact sounds. The most transferable web ideas are a bottom-weighted title-card composition, restrained but deliberate typography reveals, layered parallax over cinematic imagery, and interaction timing that feels snappy rather than floaty.

## IMAGINE Studio Adaptation

IMAGINE keeps its slower luxury pacing rather than copying the reference's ultra-fast rhythm. The site uses a cinematic hero reel, left-anchored title-card copy, aperture rings as a recurring geometry, section numbers and production-credit-like labels, and an optional ambient soundtrack with an explicit sound toggle. The research informed the motion vocabulary but did not introduce any external video or copyrighted media into the site.
